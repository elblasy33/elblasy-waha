How it works
Your Odoo, your WAHA, your WhatsApp number
Odoo calls the WAHA REST API to send and manage sessions. WAHA pushes incoming messages and status changes back to Odoo through a signed webhook, or Odoo fetches them every minute when the webhook cannot reach it.
Everything in one app
What you can do
Tap a group to open or close it.
Sessions & Inbox
Sessions with QR login
Create, start, stop, restart and log out WAHA sessions. The real status always comes from WAHA.
Secure webhook
HMAC signature check, automatic configuration, retries and idempotent processing: nothing is applied twice.
Live inbox
Conversations, unread counters, search and delivery status. Incoming photos and files are saved and shown in the chat.
Send text and files
Images, video, audio and documents up to 15 MB from the chat, with the typed text as caption.
Contact matching
Linked to Odoo contacts by phone number. LID chats are resolved to a number when WAHA knows it.
WhatsApp labels
Filter by label and tag chats from Odoo; changes are written to your WhatsApp Business account.
Groups
Sync groups with pictures
Subject, description, admin-only flags and group pictures, fetched in small batches to respect WhatsApp.
Whitelist
Odoo posts only to the groups you explicitly allow. Everything else stays read-only.
Send documents
From any invoice, order or delivery: text plus the PDF report to the customer's number and to their groups at once.
Customer-linked groups
Attach groups to a contact; documents sent to that customer also reach those groups.
Create and manage
Create groups, add or remove members, promote admins, invite links, leave a group.
Optional message log
Store messages written in a group only if you opt in; purged after 90 days by default.
Automation
Odoo automation rules
The server action Send WhatsApp (WAHA) works with any trigger: confirmation, posting, stage change.
Placeholders and reports
Write messages like “Hello {{ object.partner_id.name }}” and attach the document PDF.
Throttled queue
Messages leave slowly, with delay, once-per-record, retry and a full audit. Protects your number.
Number verification
Checks that a number has WhatsApp before sending, and respects WhatsApp's limits for new contacts.
Opt-out
Customers can reply “stop” (or “إيقاف”): automatic messages to them are blocked.
Instant messages
One click from contacts, employees and documents, with the number filled in for you.
Service & Reports
Auto-replies
First message, keywords or outside business hours, with a cooldown so it never spams.
Reject calls
Decline incoming calls and answer with your message, once per hour per caller. Calls are logged.
Activity and response time
Graph and pivot of messages per day, session and agent, plus the average response time.
Read state
Reading a chat in Odoo clears its unread badge on the phone.
Auto-sync without webhook
Testing locally? Fetch new messages every minute instead of waiting for the webhook.
WhatsApp limits
Shows the message quota and the “new contacts” lock reported by WhatsApp for the session.

Before you start
Requirements and limits
Requirements
✓Odoo 20.0 with contacts mail bus base_automation
✓Python package requests
✓A WAHA server reachable from Odoo (API key recommended, HTTPS in production)
✓For instant incoming messages: an Odoo address that WAHA can call (webhook)
✓Optional bridge: Odoo Enterprise whatsapp app
Good to know
!WAHA engines differ (WEBJS, NOWEB, GOWS). WEBJS is unofficial: avoid bulk campaigns, WhatsApp may restrict numbers.
!Phone numbers need the country code. Chats known only by LID cannot always be matched to a contact.
!The built-in inbox sends text and files; polls, buttons and message editing are not included yet.
!API keys are stored in the database in clear text, visible only to managers: protect your backups.
!Auto-sync is a fallback with up to one minute delay, not a replacement for the webhook.
FAQ
Questions
Do I need a public URL?
For instant incoming messages, yes: WAHA must be able to call your Odoo. Without it, sending works and Auto-sync can fetch new messages every minute.
Can I test it on my computer?
Yes. Point it at your WAHA server, link a number and switch on Auto-sync on the session. Everything else works the same.
Does it work on Odoo Community?
The main app does. Only the optional bridge needs the Enterprise WhatsApp app.
Does it replace Odoo's own WhatsApp?
No. Use this app alone, or add the bridge so Odoo's native WhatsApp runs over your WAHA session.
Upgrading from the previous separate modules?
Upgrade the app: a migration moves the data of the former Groups, Automation and Employees add-ons into it. No data is lost.
