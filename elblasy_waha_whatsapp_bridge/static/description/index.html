How it works
Same app, another road
Odoo's WhatsApp app normally calls Meta's Cloud API. The bridge routes those calls to your WAHA session instead and turns WAHA's webhook into the events Odoo already understands. Accounts that still use Meta keep working unchanged next to it.
Features
What changes in Odoo's WhatsApp app
Sending — everything Odoo sends goes through WAHA
Free text
Write to any number from Discuss at any time — no template, no 24 h window.
Templates still work
Approved-template screens keep working: the template is rendered in Odoo (variables, header text) and sent as a normal message; header documents and images are attached.
Media
Images, videos, voice notes and documents, with captions.
Replies
Replying to a message in Discuss sends a real quoted WhatsApp reply.
New chat with anyone
"New Chat (Discuss)" opens a WhatsApp conversation from a number or a contact, optionally with a first message.
Composer without template
From any record, send free text to up to 20 recipients.
Receiving — WhatsApp in Discuss, in real time
Messages & media
Texts, photos, videos, voice notes, documents, locations and contact cards arrive in the right Discuss channel.
Reactions & ticks
Reactions and sent / delivered / read statuses update the messages in Discuss.
Replies typed on the phone
Messages sent from the phone, WhatsApp Web or the WAHA inbox appear in the Discuss chat too (never sent twice).
Hidden numbers (LID)
Chats known only by WhatsApp's hidden id are resolved to the phone number when WAHA knows it.
Safe by design — nothing else changes
Per session
Enable or disable the bridge on a WAHA session; disabling archives the account and enabling again reuses it with its templates.
Meta accounts untouched
Only accounts linked to a WAHA session are routed through WAHA.
Groups stay in the WAHA app
Group messages keep their own features in Elblasy WhatsApp.
Upgrade guard
If a future Odoo version renames a patched method, the log says so clearly.
Counted in reports
Template sends appear in Reporting ▸ Sent Documents of the WAHA app.

Questions
Good to know
Do I need a Meta Business account?
No. The WhatsApp account created by the bridge is linked to your WAHA session; no Meta credentials are used.
Do my approved templates still work?
Yes. They are rendered by Odoo and sent as text (plus their header document or image), so the customer receives the same content.
Can I use the WAHA inbox and Discuss together?
Yes. Both show the same conversations; Discuss also shows replies typed on the phone.
Why does a chat show a number instead of a name?
WhatsApp sometimes hides numbers (LID). The bridge resolves them when WAHA can; otherwise the chat keeps the hidden id until the number is known.

ماذا يفعل الجسر؟

يجعل تطبيق واتساب الأصلي في أودو Enterprise (المناقشة، القوالب، السجل) يعمل عبر جلسة WAHA بدلًا من واجهة ميتا.

الإرسال: رسائل حرة لأي رقم في أي وقت بدون قالب وبدون نافذة 24 ساعة، القوالب المعتمدة تعمل وتُرسل كنص مع مرفق الترويسة، صور وفيديو وصوت ومستندات، والرد على رسالة يصل كرد حقيقي في واتساب. شاشة "دردشة جديدة" لمراسلة أي رقم، وإرسال نص حر من أي سجل حتى 20 مستلمًا.

الاستقبال: الرسائل والوسائط والمواقع وجهات الاتصال تصل في محادثة المناقشة الصحيحة فورًا، مع التفاعلات وعلامات التسليم والقراءة. الردود التي تكتبها من الهاتف أو واتساب ويب تظهر أيضًا في المناقشة بدون إرسالها مرة ثانية.

الأمان: يعمل لكل جلسة على حدة، وحسابات ميتا الأخرى لا تتأثر، والمجموعات تبقى في تطبيق Elblasy WhatsApp.
