Odoo WhatsApp Bridge for WAHA
Run Odoo's own WhatsApp app (Discuss, templates, chatter) through your WAHA session, and message anyone without templates.

How it works
Native Odoo WhatsApp, WAHA transport
The bridge swaps only the transport of Odoo's WhatsApp app: messages that would go to Meta's Cloud API go to your WAHA session, and WAHA events are translated into the format the native app already understands. Odoo's own code is not modified.
Everything the bridge adds
What you get
Tap a group to open or close it.
Use the native app
One click to enable
Open a session and press Enable Odoo WhatsApp: a linked WhatsApp account is created for you. Press Disable to unlink it.
Discuss and chatter
Conversations appear in Discuss > WhatsApp and on the document chatter, with delivery and read status.
Templates
Approved templates are rendered locally and sent as text, with the PDF header attached. Submitting a template approves it instantly.
Native modules keep working
Sale, accounting, follow-ups, payment links, stock and POS notifications use the same flow.
Message anyone
New Chat (Discuss)
Open a conversation with any contact or phone number from the WhatsApp menu or the contact's WhatsApp Chat button.
Free text from any chatter
The standard WhatsApp button no longer asks for a template: write a message, up to 20 recipients at a time.
No 24-hour window
Conversations stay open for replies: Meta's customer-care window does not exist with WAHA.
Sending and receiving
Media and replies
Text, images, video, audio, documents, reactions and replies in both directions.
Location and more
Incoming locations, reactions and contact messages are handled by the native app.
Webhook or Sync
Instant through the webhook, or press Sync Messages / switch on Auto-sync when Odoo cannot be reached.
LID and BSUID
Chats identified by LID are resolved to a phone number when WAHA knows it, otherwise handled through Odoo's BSUID support.
Safe by design
Meta accounts untouched
Only accounts linked to a WAHA session use WAHA. Every other WhatsApp account behaves exactly as before.
Permissions
Enabling and disabling needs the WhatsApp administrator group of Odoo.
Careful with bulk
Free text is capped at 20 recipients; for more use templates with the automation queue of the main app.

Before you start
Requirements and limits
Requirements
✓Odoo Enterprise 20.0 with the whatsapp app and whatsapp_identifiers
✓The main app Elblasy WhatsApp (WAHA) with a connected session
✓For instant incoming messages: an Odoo address that WAHA can call, otherwise use Sync Messages
Good to know
!Buttons and list messages are not supported by WEBJS: they are sent as numbered text.
!Blocking numbers from the native blocklist is not performed on WhatsApp through WAHA.
!Messages you type on the phone itself are not imported into Discuss.
!Group chats are handled by the main app, not by Discuss.
!Template statuses are local: nothing is submitted to Meta.
FAQ
Questions
Is Odoo Enterprise really required?
Yes, this add-on drives the Enterprise WhatsApp app. The main app works on Community without it.
Will my current Meta accounts break?
No. Only the account created by Enable Odoo WhatsApp uses WAHA.
Why can I message people without a template?
The template and 24-hour rules belong to Meta's Cloud API. Through WAHA they do not apply, but WhatsApp's own anti-spam limits still do.
Where do incoming messages go?
To Discuss > WhatsApp and the related document, exactly like the native app.
